Overview

Balanced temporal transport is the hidden requirement behind coherent video.

Motivation

Existing training-free methods mostly strengthen cross-frame attention or inspect local entropy, but they do not reveal whether temporal interactions stay in a healthy transport regime. We instead view temporal attention as a transport operator that should remain balanced over time.

Diagnostic

Spectral Tension compares local attention diffuseness with global spectral diversity. Negative values indicate fragmented transport, positive values indicate over-mixing, and values near zero represent better-balanced temporal states.

Intervention

Spectral Transport Homeostasis is a training-free query-temperature regulator that softly sharpens over-mixed states, softens fragmented ones, and largely preserves already balanced transport.

Method at a glance

Diagnose temporal imbalance, then correct only the pathological states.

Our method introduces a signed diagnostic, Spectral Tension, to identify two opposite temporal failures: fragmented transport and over-mixing hotspots. Based on this diagnosis, Spectral Transport Homeostasis applies selective inference-time regulation without trainable parameters.

  • Preserves balanced temporal states with minimal perturbation.
  • Applies larger corrections to the worst temporal hotspots.
  • Improves temporal consistency and visual quality without finetuning.
